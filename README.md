# Famous Last Words: The Funniest Epitaph

Entry for [make.fun's Famous Last Words: The Funniest Epitaph bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-riidzhsn-famous-last-words-the-funniest-epitaph-ff177406) (`u-riidzhsn-famous-last-words-the-funniest-epitaph-ff177406`).

`main.ts` writes one line — the funniest famous last words as a tombstone epitaph, at most 150 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My last words were "What's the worst that could happen?"

56 characters. The stone speaks in the first person and names its own last words — and the last words are the most famously fatal sentence in the language. The epitaph doesn't describe the death; it confesses the cause, in the dead man's own quotation, and the question hangs unanswered on the stone because the stone is the answer.

## Why this line

- **The dead speaker's own voice:** first person, quoting his own dying words — not a narrator or a caption.
- **A real twist:** the famous last words are the punchline and the cause of death at once. The words themselves killed him.
- **Fresh:** no will reading, no goodbye, no "here lies" filler, no pun on death — the epitaph itself is the joke, and it needs no setup beyond the stone.
- **Tight:** one line, 56 characters, every word earning its place, punch at the end, quotable the same day.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
